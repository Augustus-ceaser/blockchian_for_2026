# Phase 5.13C Local Data Quality

The minimum profile records assessment scope, method version, count disclosure,
completeness, uniqueness, consistency, validity, timeliness, missingness,
format, coding, limitations, warnings, and fitness-for-use status.

No scalar quality score or `high_quality=true` claim exists. The demonstration
profile was created by `local.curator` and approved by the distinct
`local.reviewer`. Its `locally_reviewed` decision is limited to the named public
metadata fixture and is not clinical fitness, general quality certification,
legal approval, or proof of anonymization.
